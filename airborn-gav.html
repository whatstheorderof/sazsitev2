Airborn Gav

East London based musician. Subtle tones and a deeper soul.

SpotifyBandcampInstagramTwitter

Terminal Free: Departures

It’s been a good yet challenging year for him but he has not allowed those obstacles to get in the way of his destination in the music scene. Terminal Free is Airborn Gav’s best depiction of his free state of mind, his view on music, and where he stands as an emerging artist.

He has a history of revamping and taking a different approach to his past projects, T.H.C: The Hustler’s Coma and R.N.B, for example with “Take It Slow” off of the T.H.C project, later revamped to the current version out now on SoundCloud. Terminal Free features up and coming artists such as Nipah, Spacey Blak, J Flowz and Selvsse.
