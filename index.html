The SAZ Project

Every song we release features a single cover made by a creative: a photographer, painter, illustrator, designer, writer or tattoo artist. As people hear the music, they see the work.

Creatives who are featured regularly get their own page and a place in the directory, so the people in the community can find each other and make things together. Read more about SAZ.
