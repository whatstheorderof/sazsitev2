About

The start

SAZ started in West London as a duo: Zane Morris-Stewart and Seon Rice-Lupin. Drawing on acoustic, hip-hop, soul, jazz and ambient music, they built soundscapes out of whatever they could imagine, beginning with the Take Time EP in 2012 and How You Feel in 2013.

In 2013 they made Dive with the artist Dree Velicaria, writing one track a week for seven weeks to go with each new image. The finished works were shown at the Richmond Upon Thames Art and Design Exhibition that summer.

The band

When life took Seon in a different direction, Zane began making music with Kelvin Bueno. The sound moved away from hip-hop jazz towards something more eclectic, with a heavier pull from folk and indie, across Journey (2014), Chances (2015) and After Dark (2016).

SAZ grew into a five-piece live band, alongside acoustic sets built live on a loop pedal. Red Bull London named them one to watch, and GRM Daily featured Scum, the second single from Chances. Transitions (2017), Dread Full (2018), The Great Escape and 22 Reasons To Stay (2019) and Sky Roads (2020) followed, with verses from Joe James, Miraa May, Airborn Gav and Kojey Radical along the way.

The duo made music together until 2021. Kelvin now performs as a separate act, Sounds Mint.

Now

Today SAZ is Zane's solo project. He writes, produces and releases from London on SAZ Records: Dread Full II in 2022, then Changing Tides, and in 2025 the Delusions of Grandeur EP and With/Out You.

The SAZ Project

Alongside the music, SAZ built a community whose common language is art and music. We have constructed a self sustaining community, whose common language is art and music. Every song that we release features a ‘Single’ cover, compiled of a creative's work. We feel that in working together, we can create the perfect medium to get people's work seen by new audiences.

The creative (photographer, painter, illustrator, designer, writer, tattoo artist, etc) can send in their work, and if used, develops an initial link between themselves and our community. As people hear our music, they will see your work. Not only does it make the creative feel involved, it acts as a pathway to broadcasting your artistry.

The artist will receive their own ‘SAZ Creatives page’ if regularly featured, showcasing their work and links to their relevant materials, whilst also being added to our rapidly expanding directory. This means that if you’re a photographer looking for, say, a make-up artist, you could refer to our listings, find an artist whose work appeals to you, and form further links for yourselves.

A community of talent, all joined under our SAZ umbrella.
