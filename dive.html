2013

Dive

SAZ × Dree Velicaria. A soundtrack album made with a graphic artist, one week at a time.

Over seven weeks, Dree Velicaria made an image each week and we wrote a track to go with it, working the way an anime soundtrack would.

The finished works were shown at the Richmond Upon Thames Art and Design Exhibition in July 2013. The album is a free download on SoundCloud.
